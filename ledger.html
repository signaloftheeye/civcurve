Rule v3 summary (adds to v2)

  • Entry record: probability, market price with timestamp (ET), resolution source and exact wording, a verbatim quote of the line the office must or cannot say with its register row id, falsifier, and an event id.
  • Inversion step: every "normal procedure will happen" call must state what the office gains by not acting before the date. If that gain is real and dated inside the window, downgrade or flip the call.
  • One resolution clause per row. Anything that depends on our own filing is a task, not a forecast.
  • Self/meta forecasts go in a separate ledger and are never traded.
  • Trend exit: when a weekly score marks the trend "away" on a verified source, exit or post an updated probability.
  • Override check: stated probability is capped at 60% if a court, statute, other branch, or counterparty can force the outcome.
  • Edge gate: price recorded, and a gap of 10 points or more against the ask.
  • Warning signs before entry (drop the tier or skip): a court, statute, or license date inside the window; 2+ anonymous sources contradicting the line; the trend turning; the market resolving on a number.
  • v2 limits still apply: tier caps, ¼ Kelly, $25 max, $200 bankroll, 4 open, correlated positions count as one.
