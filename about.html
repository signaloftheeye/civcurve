About & method

An open register, read like an instrument

The Civilization Curve is a public instrument by Signal (@signaloftheeye). It tracks power at the level of named people and offices. Every row in the open register has a date, a grade, a SAID or DONE mark, a source and a falsifier.

SAID vs DONE

SAID

What a named person or office stated: a speech, a post, a briefing, an interview.

DONE

What a named office executed and left a trace of: an order, a signature, a filing, a strike, a market print.

The register keeps the two apart. The gap between what is said and what is done is the thing being measured.

Grades

V

Verified

The row matches a primary or official trace.

I

Inference

Reasoned from verified rows, and labelled as inference.

TK

To come

Attributed, but the primary trace is not yet in hand. Held open until the falsifier settles it.

UNVERIFIED

Unverified

Circulating, with no trace found yet.

REFUTED

Refuted

Checked against the trace and found false.

How the curves are computed

World entropy = 70% world harm + 30% market stress. World harm counts verified (V or I) DONE rows from the last 30 days that describe strikes or attacks, seizures, shutdowns or ground stops, bans or sanctions, or deaths, plus UKMTO incident rows, uncapped. Market stress is each feed's position between a fixed calm level and a fixed stress level (VIX, Brent, US 10Y, DXY, SPY/RSP breadth), plus gold read two-sided: a fast fall counts as stress too.

World restoration counts the same kind of rows that describe world-side repair: a court ruling enforced, a release, a ceasefire holding, an official record corrected, or accountability. Both world lines use one fixed baseline (score = 100 × rows ÷ (rows + 320)), so they can be compared and a sustained rise shows as a rise.

Our register line. Our own grading quality (share graded V and share not unverified or refuted) is drawn as a separate grey line. Our own process rows (checks, corrections, reviews, AI tests, power-map ties, own posts) are kept out of both world lines.

Method change, Nov 14 2026. The old indices (one-year market percentiles plus our own grading shares, with a 30-day straight-line projection) are retired. Every point is recomputed under the new method and the change is marked on the chart. Baselines, bands and word lists →

Register discipline. Rows are appended one at a time. Nothing is deleted. Corrections are filed as retracts and shown struck through; a fired falsifier is logged when the observation that would prove a row wrong actually happens.

Feeds. Yahoo Finance chart API (BZZ26.NYM, ^VIX, SPY, RSP, ^TNX, GC=F, DX-Y.NYB) and the Japan Ministry of Finance (JGB 10Y). Bands come from WINDOW.md.

Not advice. Nothing here is trading or investment advice. The indices are descriptive.
