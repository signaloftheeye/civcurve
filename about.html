About & method

An open register, read like an instrument

The Civilization Curve is a public instrument by Signal (@signaloftheeye). It tracks power at the level of named people and offices. Every row in the open register has a date, a grade, a SAID or DONE mark, a source and a kill condition.

SAID vs DONE

SAID

What a named person or office stated: a speech, a post, a briefing, an interview.

DONE

What a named office executed and left a trace of: an order, a signature, a filing, a strike, a market print.

The register keeps the two apart. The gap between what is said and what is done is the thing being measured.

Grades

V

Verified

The row matches a primary or official trace.

I

Inference

Reasoned from verified rows, and labelled as inference.

TK

To come

Attributed, but the primary trace is not yet in hand. Held open until the kill condition settles it.

UNVERIFIED

Unverified

Circulating, with no trace found yet.

REFUTED

Refuted

Checked against the trace and found false.

How the curves are computed

Collapse = 60% market stress + 20% stress-theatre share + 20% UKMTO intensity. Market stress is the mean one-year percentile rank of Brent (Dec), VIX, SPY/RSP (narrow breadth), the UST 10Y yield, gold and DXY. The stress-theatre share is the share of the last 30 days of register rows in force, Hormuz, energy, Iran, markets or money. UKMTO intensity is the number of register rows citing UKMTO in the last 30 days, capped at 40.

Restoration = 30% share of V rows + 20% DONE share of SAID/DONE rows + 20% calm volatility (1 − VIX percentile) + 15% broad breadth (1 − SPY/RSP percentile) + 15% (1 − share of UNVERIFIED and REFUTED rows), all over 30 days.

Projection. A 30-day straight-line fit to the last 20 sessions, with an error band that widens over time. It shows momentum only.

Register discipline. Rows are appended one at a time. Nothing is deleted. Corrections are filed as retracts and shown struck through; kills are filed when a row's kill condition is met.

Feeds. Yahoo Finance chart API (BZZ26.NYM, ^VIX, SPY, RSP, ^TNX, GC=F, DX-Y.NYB) and the Japan Ministry of Finance (JGB 10Y). Bands come from WINDOW.md.

Not advice. Nothing here is trading or investment advice. The indices are descriptive.
