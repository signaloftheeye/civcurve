How to read it. Each link is one documented tie: a family tie, a job, a payment, a contract, an appointment, a meeting. V = verified against a primary or reputable source; I = partly supported. A sequence, no cause claimed link records two documented facts in time order and nothing more. Context ties are named inside register rows and have no row of their own. No tie on this map implies intent or causation.
