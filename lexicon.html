Lexicon

The working vocabulary

Terms used in the open register and on this site.

SAID vs DONE
Two kinds of fact. SAID is what a named person or office stated (a speech, post, briefing). DONE is what a named office executed and left a trace of (an order, signature, filing, strike, print). The register marks each row as one or the other, and the gap between them is the thing being measured.
Grade V
Verified: the row matches a primary or official trace.
Grade I
Inference: reasoned from verified rows, labelled as inference.
Grade TK
To come: the claim is attributed but the primary trace is not yet in hand. It holds open until the kill condition resolves it.
Grade UNVERIFIED
Circulating, with no trace found yet.
Grade REFUTED
Checked against the trace and found false.
Kill condition
The specific, named event that would prove a row or forecast wrong, filed at the same moment as the claim. If it happens, the row is killed and the kill is logged in public (kills.jsonl).
Retract
A filed correction. The original row stays visible, struck through, with the note explaining what changed.
Theatre
The arena a row belongs to: force, Hormuz, energy, markets, money, files, powermap, narrative, religion, softener, optics and others.
Gazette
An official trace: a government notice, an order, a filing, a named official on record. A gazette reprices a band; a caption does not.
Tape
Market prints read against filed bands.
Band
A price level filed in WINDOW.md with a reference print and a stated reversal (for example Brent Dec ≥ 100, reversed by a Friday weekly close under 100). Bands expire with the weekly window.
Window
The seven-day working file (WINDOW.md) that holds live bands. It resets on its expiry date; expired windows are archived.
Hole
A documented absence: a search that should return an official trace and returns nothing. Holes are logged with the query, a mundane and a charged phrasing, the count found and who made the hole (for example the gazette or an API), and are closed when a row fills them.
System-preserver
The test of what the powers that be attempt: when a shock lands, do named offices act to keep the existing arrangement running (calm the tape, defend the index, soften the story) instead of resolving the cause? The tracker logs VIX, breadth, volume and Brent on shock days against stated kill and confirm triggers.
Blame menu
A rotating list of causes offered by an office for an outcome that never names that office's own policy. Logged with a kill window like any other claim.
Softener
Language or framing from an office or outlet that lowers the felt cost of a DONE without changing it, such as elevating a denial over a document.
Selection-beneficiary
Who gains if a given line is believed, logged as inference beside the claim.
Ladder
How far a row has climbed toward a full trace (for example from a caption, to an attributed count, to a named person).
Collapse curve
On this site: a 0–100 index of measured stress, made from market percentiles plus the register's stress-theatre share and UKMTO mentions.
Restoration curve
On this site: a 0–100 index of measured repair, made from the register's verified and DONE shares plus calm volatility and broad breadth.
